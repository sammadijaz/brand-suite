# legal boundaries and localisation

These are substantive proposed drafts, not advice from a licensed lawyer or universal enforceability assurances. Determine actual entity/jurisdiction through appropriate sources; employee work location, counterparty and processing destinations require separate assessment. No default Pakistani, US or EU regime applies.

During each use consult current official sources for material mandatory-law assertions, employment entitlements, notice/service, tax invoice fields, stamp duty, execution/witnessing/electronic signatures and transfers. Record applicability, location/date and interpretation limits; official publication alone does not complete legal analysis. If sources are unavailable, retain a precise draft/review gate and question.

Do not invent wages, leave, tax rates, deductions, duration, venue, caps, indemnities, payment periods, IP title or incident deadlines. Select intentionally supplied terms or visible required fields. Preserve mandatory rights and protected disclosures. Contractor labels do not decide classification. NDA does not authorize data processing. DPA commitments must match actual controls and providers. MOU binding clauses are express; execution sheets attach to identified versions. Certified-copy records remain unexecuted until genuine comparison by an authorized certifier.

Human states and technical states are separate. Local counsel, accountant, prescribed forms and recipient mandates may be required. Legal commentary belongs in the review pack; removing comments cannot remove substantive protections.
