# stamp production

Ordinary company/signatory stamps are identifiers; they do not confer authority or constitute an adopted common seal. Configurable starting geometry is 40 mm circle and 62×25 mm rectangle. Long names wrap at readable scale or require larger approved dimensions; no silent lettering redesign.

Supply outlined SVG, exact-size PDF, true 600 dpi transparent raster and editable JSON dimensions/text/font specification. Font files are not packaged. Positive impression is default; mirror only under explicit fabricator instruction. Proposed 0.3 mm line/gap floor is a design starting point; supplier material/ink/laser limitations and physical proof must be recorded.

Print at 100 percent; compare dimensions against a ruler during real proof. A page-box check is digital dimensional verification, not a physical stamp test. No official emblem, handwriting, certification, notary insignia, fake registration number or adopted seal is created. Assign custodian and log each authorized use independently from document release.
