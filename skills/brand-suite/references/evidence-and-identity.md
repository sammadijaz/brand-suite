# evidence and identity

Keep legal_name, trading_name, entity_type, jurisdiction, registered_office, mailing_address, registration_identifier, tax_identifier, contacts, signatory and authority separate. Select company.entity_id and match the input entity_id. Never infer incorporation from a domain, authority from a Git author, a registered office from a personal address or a company tax ID from a founder's number.

Each field carries value/null, evidence status, source identifier, locator, date/hash, scope and verification need. Primary identity requires official records or actual issued documents; owner confirmation is OWNER_PROVIDED, not fabricated official evidence. Approved masters establish brand choices. Implementation establishes behavior at reviewed scope; public statements establish claims only. Proposed terms remain PROPOSED.

UNKNOWN uses null; CONFLICTED may preserve candidate values but cannot resolve into a factual assertion. Preserve conflicts and owner/source-backed resolutions. No automated confidence promotion. Verify evidence-map/source-register consistency and required namespaces. Public synthetic fixtures explicitly mark fictional=true and source kind synthetic; those cannot verify real identity.
