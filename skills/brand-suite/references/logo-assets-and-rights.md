# logo assets and rights

Preserve originals byte-for-byte and hash them. Label every recolour, crop, trace, reconstruction or rasterization. Prefer genuine approved source vectors; an embedded raster is not a vector master. Never claim recovered original geometry from a PNG.

The helper accepts deliberately limited passive SVGs: no scripts, event attributes, external/embedded resources, foreignObject, animation or complex effect references. Unsupported art needs a safe, owner-approved conversion in an isolated tool; it is not silently altered. Preserve source proportions. Final stamp lettering is outlined and independent from editable source specs; body text remains native.

No fonts or third-party trademark library are distributed. Confirm rights for input artwork, font logo/outline use and renderer embedding where applicable. A repository software licence does not grant third-party trademark or artwork rights. No trademark clearance, endorsement or affiliation is claimed. Missing approved art allows a visibly provisional typographic treatment.
