# legal scope

This repository authors substantive proposed clauses and review controls. It is not a licensed legal service and does not certify enforceability. Facts, business choices, legal requirements and reviewer annotations remain separate. Consult current applicable official sources during actual use; location/date/applicability belong in evidence.

No inferred incorporation, office, tax ID, authority, wages, leave, notice, jurisdiction, cap, duration, incident deadline or IP title. Missing facts render visible typed fields. A project stays a project; governance is a conditional note when inapplicable. Employment location, contractor classification, processor roles and transfers require separate assessment. Protected disclosures and mandatory-law carveouts remain.

Certified-copy drafts are unexecuted. Ordinary stamps are not adopted seals. Commercial invoices are not asserted statutory tax invoices. Email signatures are not execution signatures. Technical passes and synthetic VERIFIED_PRIMARY fields do not establish human or legal approval.
