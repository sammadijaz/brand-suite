# design system

House defaults are restrained: white pages, 22 mm sides, reserved header/footer zones, 11 pt body and 9 pt footer, two or fewer type families and modest identifiers. A4/Letter paper choice is independent of law. Clear space follows an actual mark element; minimum size follows reviewed specimens, not an arbitrary score.

The illustrated catalogue contains actual generated logo variants, clear-space/size ladders, palette/type specimen and page anatomy. Provisional typography is used if no approved logo exists. The approved source is preserved; derivatives record geometry preservation and recolours. Only passive SVG is accepted by the automated path. Raster source, opaque corners, complex SVG or low effective resolution require an explicitly limited draft/safe approved conversion rather than a false vector master.

HEX/RGB are screen values. Contrast is a mathematical check, not accessibility certification. Print colour conversions, Pantone, fabrication materials and physical proofs require real supplier review. Body text remains native and searchable. No fonts or example trademarks are bundled.
